How costs are kept low

  • Cheapest suitable model (4-step flux/schnell), one image per idea
  • Images are cached: re-running never pays twice (--force --only to redo one)
  • Text-free art, words drawn in Remotion, so fewer regenerations
  • Everything heavy (transcription, rendering) runs locally for free
  • Cost logged per image in data/visuals_manifest.json
